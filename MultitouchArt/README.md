# MultitouchArt iOS App

Complete iOS application with multitouch support, procedural sounds, and vibrant visual effects.

## Quick Start

To build and deploy this app to TestFlight:

1. Open `MultitouchArt/MultitouchArt.xcodeproj` in Xcode
2. Configure your Apple Developer Team in Signing & Capabilities
3. Archive the app (Product > Archive)
4. Upload to App Store Connect
5. Configure TestFlight and invite testers

See `README_MULTITOUCHART.md` for detailed instructions.

## App Features

- Multitouch particle system with 11 vibrant colors
- Procedural audio synthesis with harmonic tones
- Haptic feedback on touch
- Smooth trails and gradient effects
- Works on all iOS devices and orientations
